HOW TO APPLY FOR A JOB:
1. Log in to your Worker account.
2. Go to the "Recommended Jobs" dashboard.
3. Use the search bar or distance filter to find a job that matches your skills.
4. Click on the job card to view details (wage, location, date).
5. Click the "Apply" button. 
6. The employer will review your profile. You will get a notification if you are shortlisted.

HOW TO POST A REQUIREMENT (FOR EMPLOYERS):
1. Log in to your Employer account.
2. Click the "+" button on the bottom navigation bar.
3. Fill in the job details: Title, Skill Required, Number of Workers, Date, and Wage.
4. Set the location using the map or by typing the village/city name.
5. Click "Post Requirement". The matching engine will instantly show you a list of suitable workers.

HOW TO UPDATE YOUR PROFILE:
1. Go to the "Profile" tab.
2. Click "Edit Profile".
3. You can add new skills, update your expected wage, or change your availability dates.
4. Click "Save" to ensure the matching engine uses your latest information.
