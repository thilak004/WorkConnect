How to apply for a job:
1. Go to 'Browse Jobs' from the navigation bar.
2. Filter jobs by your trade or location.
3. Click 'Apply Now' on any open job listing.

How to post a requirement:
1. Sign in as an Employer.
2. Click 'Post a Job' in the top navigation.
3. Fill in job title, required skill, daily wage, and location, then click submit.

How to update a worker profile:
1. Go to your Worker Dashboard.
2. Click 'Edit Profile & Preferences'.
3. Update your skill, experience, wage expectations, or phone number and save changes.
